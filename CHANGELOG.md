## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added

- Heat pumps with a Luxtronik 2.0 or 2.1 controller (Alpha Innotec, Novelan, Siemens, Buderus, Nibe AP, Roth, Elco, Wolf) read on the local network over the binary socket of the controller (TCP port 8889), one connection at a time, closed after each reading, every 30 seconds to 5 minutes.
- Heat pumps reached at the IP addresses typed in the configuration, plus the Alpha Innotec network search (UDP 4444 and 47808) sent through Gladys; identified by their serial number, so a new address is followed.
- Up to four devices per heat pump: the heat pump (outdoor, flow, return, hot gas and heat source temperatures, room temperature with a room unit, operating mode, status, last error, compressor, backup heater, electrical power and energy used, heat output and heat produced when the controller reports them, compressor hours and starts, backup heater hours), heating (mode, temperature +/-), hot water (temperature, target, mode, heating now) and cooling (on / off, target) when the heat pump has them.
- Changes (modes, temperature +/-, hot water target, cooling) off by default: once allowed, a value already set is never written again, one setting at most every 2 minutes, 50 changes a day at most, within fixed bounds, read back after writing; firmware V1.90 and V2.90 stay read only; the hot water target goes to the register the firmware follows.
- Dashboard widgets "Heat pump" (mode, compressor, temperatures, power), "Heat pump curves" (outdoor, flow, return and hot water temperatures over 1, 3 or 7 days), "Heat pump energy" (heat produced, electricity used, seasonal performance, compressor hours and starts, backup heater hours) and "Heat pump log" (errors and compressor stops with their dates).
- Scene triggers: an error is reported, the operating mode changes (heating, hot water, defrost, grid lock, cooling...), the backup heater switches on or off, the heat pump stops answering or answers again.
- Scene action: read the heat pump (mode, temperatures, power, last error).
- Configuration button "Search and test the heat pumps".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
